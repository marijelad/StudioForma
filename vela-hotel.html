Case study

Designing the essential.

01 · Challenge

The property needed a distinctive identity that avoided familiar nautical clichés.

02 · Response

We translated coastal references into proportion, texture and shadow rather than literal motifs, pairing pale stone with woven textiles and oxidized metal.

03 · Outcome

A sophisticated hotel identity that feels rooted in place and consistent from lobby to guestrooms.

A restrained palette lets grain, reflection, shadow and touch create the atmosphere.
