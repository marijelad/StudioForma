Monolith Villa

A highly edited villa defined by monolithic surfaces, precise openings and almost invisible storage.

LocationAthens, Greece
Area336 m²
Year2025
Indicative build budget€780K
Case study

Designing the essential.

01 · Challenge

The client wanted minimalism without sterility, while accommodating a large art and book collection.

02 · Response

We embedded storage within thickened walls, used warm limestone and oak, and treated artworks as focal points within long sightlines.

03 · Outcome

A disciplined home with visual calm, tactile depth and enough flexibility for the collection to evolve.

Material study

Texture before decoration.

A restrained palette lets grain, reflection, shadow and touch create the atmosphere.
