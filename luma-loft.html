Case study

Designing the essential.

01 · Challenge

The existing shell was dramatic but dark, with poor zoning and little usable storage.

02 · Response

We introduced a freestanding timber core for utilities, preserved the brick perimeter, and created layered lighting and linen-textured surfaces.

03 · Outcome

An open loft that feels expansive by day and intimate at night, with flexible zones for work, hosting and rest.
