Case study

Designing the essential.

01 · Challenge

The existing interiors felt generic and disconnected from the landscape.

02 · Response

We reorganized public areas around fireplaces and view axes, then introduced local stone, charred timber and handwoven textiles.

03 · Outcome

A more intimate guest experience with strong regional character and a clear winter-to-summer material story.
