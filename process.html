Process

A clear path through complex decisions.

Interior projects contain hundreds of linked choices. Our process organizes them into visible stages so clients know what is being decided, why and when.

01
Discover
We map goals, constraints, routines, site conditions and decision priorities.
02
Define
We establish spatial principles, references, budget ranges and the design brief.
03
Design
Plans, elevations, materials, lighting and custom elements develop together.
04
Detail
We resolve junctions, specifications, schedules and information for pricing or construction.
05
Deliver
We support procurement, site questions, installation and final styling.
Decision discipline
Every stage ends with something concrete to approve.

That might be a plan, a material direction, a cost checkpoint or a coordinated drawing package. The purpose is not bureaucracy; it is to protect the design from late, expensive uncertainty.
