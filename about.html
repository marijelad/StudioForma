About StudioForma

Clarity, craft and human rhythm.

Founded as a small interior architecture practice, StudioForma works across homes, hospitality and workplaces with one goal: make spaces feel inevitable rather than styled.

Good interiors support behavior quietly.

We begin with how a space needs to perform: where people arrive, pause, work, host, recover and store the ordinary things of life. From there, proportion, material and light are developed as one connected language.

Our portfolio demo represents a fictional European design practice created to showcase a complete multi-page website experience.

Principles we return to.

01

Use before image

Plans are tested against routines before decorative decisions begin.

02

Fewer, better materials

We prefer a concise palette with depth, repairability and graceful aging.

03

Precision without rigidity

Clear details should support daily life, not make users anxious about the space.
